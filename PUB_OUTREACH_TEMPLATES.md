## Tips for Success

1. **Personalize:** Always use pub name and specific details
2. **Keep it short:** Busy pub managers don't read long emails
3. **Clear CTA:** One action - reply or click link
4. **Social proof:** Mention competitors who joined
5. **FOMO:** Limited spots, early adopter benefits
6. **Free trial:** 3 months FREE removes risk
7. **Make it easy:** Offer to do it for them

**Best times to email pubs:**
- Tuesday-Thursday (avoid Monday chaos, Friday/weekend rush)
- 10am-2pm (after morning prep, before evening rush)

**Best times to call:**
- Tuesday-Thursday, 2-4pm

Good luck! 🍺
