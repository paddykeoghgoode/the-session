## 🎯 Sponsorship Sales Process

### Week 1: Outreach
1. **Identify 50 potential sponsors**
   - 30 pubs (mix of independent + chains)
   - 10 delivery platforms / brands
   - 5 breweries
   - 5 other (tourism boards, events, etc.)

2. **Send personalized emails**
   - Monday: 10 pubs (Template 1)
   - Tuesday: 10 pubs (Template 1)
   - Wednesday: 5 delivery platforms (Template 1)
   - Thursday: 10 pubs (Template 1)
   - Friday: Breweries + others

### Week 2: Follow-Up
3. **Follow up with non-responders**
   - Use Template 2
   - Call if no email response

4. **Schedule calls with interested parties**
   - Prepare pitch deck (slides showing traffic, demographics)
   - Show example sponsorship mockups

### Week 3: Close Deals
5. **Send agreements**
   - Use template above
   - Request logo files
   - Set up payment (bank transfer or invoice)

6. **Onboard sponsors**
   - Add logo to leaderboard
   - Schedule social posts
   - Set up prize mechanics

### Week 4: Deliver Results
7. **Execute sponsorship**
   - Post regularly featuring sponsor
   - Track impressions, clicks, engagement
   - Facilitate winner-sponsor connection

8. **Send monthly report**
   - Impressions, reach, engagement
   - Winner visit details/photos
   - Social media metrics
   - ROI calculation

## 📊 Pricing Strategy

### Start Cheap, Prove Value
**Month 1-3:** €50/month (Bronze only)
- Goal: Get 5 sponsors, prove ROI
- Over-deliver on value
- Collect testimonials

**Month 4-6:** Add Silver (€100) and Gold (€200)

- Upsell existing Bronze sponsors
- Target bigger pubs/chains

**Month 7+:** Full pricing
